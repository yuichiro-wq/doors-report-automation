## Policy

Client-facing monthly-report headlines must state the implication or decision point, not merely narrate whether a metric increased or decreased.

Do not surface internal production vocabulary in the client-facing body, including: `正本`, `SSOT`, `正式値未出力`, `完成版では未掲載`, `旧定義ログ`, `AI source contract`, `exact URL`, or workflow wording such as `シートへ転記`.

When a value exists but its definition differs from the previous month, show the current-month value and place the definition difference in a short note. Do not replace the metric with `未確定` merely because a direct month-over-month comparison is unavailable.

CV funnel values based on submit-click events must not be described as confirmed inquiries.
